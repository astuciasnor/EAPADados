Southern Oscillation Index

Description:

     Southern Oscillation Index (SOI) for a period of 453 months
     ranging over the years 1950-1987.

Format:

     The format is: Time-Series [1:453] from 1950 to 1988: 0.377 0.246
     0.311 0.104 -0.016 0.235 0.137 0.191 -0.016 0.29 ...

Details:

     pairs with 'rec' (Recruitment)

Source:

     Data furnished by Dr. Roy Mendelssohn of the Pacific Fisheries
     Environmental Laboratory, NOAA (personal communication).
