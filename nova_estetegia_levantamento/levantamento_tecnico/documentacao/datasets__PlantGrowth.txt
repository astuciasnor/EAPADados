Results from an Experiment on Plant Growth

Description:

     Results from an experiment to compare yields (as measured by dried
     weight of plants) obtained under a control and two different
     treatment conditions.

Usage:

     PlantGrowth
     
Format:

     A data frame of 30 cases on 2 variables.

       [, 1]  'weight'  numeric 
       [, 2]  'group'   factor  
      
     The levels of 'group' are 'ctrl', 'trt1', and 'trt2'.

Source:

     Dobson AJ (1983). _An Introduction to Statistical Modelling_.
     Springer, New York, NY. ISBN 978-0412248603.

Examples:

     ## One factor ANOVA example from Dobson's book, cf. Table 7.4:
     require(stats); require(graphics)
     boxplot(weight ~ group, data = PlantGrowth, main = "PlantGrowth data",
             ylab = "Dried weight of plants", col = "lightgray",
             notch = TRUE, varwidth = TRUE)
     anova(lm(weight ~ group, data = PlantGrowth))
