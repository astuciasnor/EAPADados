Chicken Weights by Feed Type

Description:

     An experiment was conducted to measure and compare the
     effectiveness of various feed supplements on the growth rate of
     chickens.

Usage:

     chickwts
     
Format:

     A data frame with 71 observations on the following 2 variables.

     'weight'
          a numeric variable giving the chick weight in grams.

     'feed'
          a factor giving the feed type.

Details:

     Newly hatched chicks were randomly allocated into six groups, and
     each group was given a different feed supplement.  Their weights
     in grams after six weeks are given along with feed types.

Source:

     Snedecor GW (1948). "60. Query." _Biometrics_, *4*(3), 213-215.
     doi:10.2307/3001566 <https://doi.org/10.2307/3001566>.

References:

     McNeil D (1977). _Interactive Data Analysis: A Practical Primer_.
     John Wiley & Sons. ISBN 978-0471026310.

Examples:

     require(stats); require(graphics)
     boxplot(weight ~ feed, data = chickwts, col = "lightgray",
         varwidth = TRUE, notch = TRUE, main = "chickwt data",
         ylab = "Weight at six weeks (gm)")
     anova(fm1 <- lm(weight ~ feed, data = chickwts))
     opar <- par(mfrow = c(2, 2), oma = c(0, 0, 1.1, 0),
                 mar = c(4.1, 4.1, 2.1, 1.1))
     plot(fm1)
     par(opar)
